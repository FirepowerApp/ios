# Firepower Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

Firepower does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads,
analytics or tracking.

## Data stored on your device

Firepower saves your pinned teams and a cached copy of today's NHL schedule on your
device. This data never leaves your device, and deleting the app removes it.

## Network requests

To show today's games, Firepower requests the public NHL schedule from the NHL's API
(api-web.nhle.com). The request doesn't include any information about you. Like any web
request, it exposes your IP address to the NHL's servers, which are covered by the NHL's
own privacy policy.

## Live Activities

Live game updates are delivered through Apple Push Notification service broadcast
channels. Your device subscribes to a team's channel through Apple, and the Firepower
server sends updates to that channel without knowing which devices receive them. No
device identifier or push token is sent to Firepower.

## Notifications

Pre-game alerts are scheduled locally on your device. Firepower does not send them from a
server.

## Children

Firepower does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective
date. The full history is available in this repository.

## Contact
